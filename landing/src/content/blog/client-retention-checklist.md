Do you remember your favorite teacher from high school? Chances are someone comes to mind — someone who made you feel good about yourself and your abilities.

That feeling is exactly what your clients should experience when they work with you. Here is a checklist of gestures — big and small — that keep clients coming back:

- **Create a sense of connection.** Be genuinely interested in their progress.
- **Go the extra mile.** A small, unexpected effort tailored to them makes a lasting impression.
- **Protect them in conversation.** Never speak negatively about them to others.
- **Make them feel safe.** Create an environment where they can be vulnerable without judgment.
- **Acknowledge their struggles.** Validate the effort they are putting in.
- **Cheer them on.** Celebrate their wins, no matter how small.
- **Compliment them early.** If you see potential in a new client, tell them. A sincere compliment in the first session sets a positive tone for the entire relationship.

Retention is not about discounts or gimmicks — it is about making people feel valued, consistently, over time.
