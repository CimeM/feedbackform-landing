CRM software today is draining the humanity out of client relationships. The sooner you recognize this, the clearer the solution becomes.

Large CRM providers discovered long ago that businesses need their software. They made it feature-rich, then addictive, then expensive. Features piled on to satisfy enterprise buyers — the people who write the checks, not the people who use the software daily. This pattern mirrors the bloatware era of early Microsoft products: one wrong move and the entire layout breaks.

CRMs should be purpose-built for the specific segment they serve. A client dashboard for a tutor or coach should not resemble a sales pipeline for a multinational corporation. The user experience should be human-friendly, not sterile and corporate.

Lérins AI was built with this philosophy in mind. We prioritize clear interfaces, intuitive workflows, and genuine human connection. Every feature exists to make relationships stronger, not to inflate a metric. We believe software should feel warm, approachable, and even fun to use.

If you have ever felt drained by a CRM, Lérins AI is the antidote.
