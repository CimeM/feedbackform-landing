Asking clients for feedback should feel natural, not awkward. It gives you visibility into how they experienced the session and gives them a sense that their opinion matters.

When you collect feedback thoughtfully and act on it, you demonstrate that you are listening and improving. This builds trust and deepens the relationship.

Lérins AI makes feedback a seamless part of your workflow. When you mark a session as complete, you can provide feedback on the client's progress and behavior. Start with what went well, then note areas for improvement. Since clients can see this feedback, keep the tone constructive and positive.

At the same time, clients and students have the opportunity to evaluate the session from their perspective. This two-way feedback loop is a cornerstone of the Lérins AI experience. Relationships are built on communication, and consistent feedback creates a documented history of growth that both parties can reference.

When it is time to celebrate a milestone, the feedback trail shows exactly how far you have come together. Every step matters — and Lérins AI helps you capture each one.
