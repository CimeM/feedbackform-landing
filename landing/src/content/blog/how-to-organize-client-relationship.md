Airtable and spreadsheets are excellent starting points for any project. They are free, flexible, and widely accessible. But they only take you so far.

As your client base grows, managing everything in a single table becomes unwieldy. You find yourself juggling multiple sheets, cross-referencing data, and spending more time maintaining the system than serving your clients. That is the moment to transition to a dedicated application — something that feels intuitive and makes organization its top priority.

Lérins AI's client dashboard is built for exactly this inflection point. Automation keeps everyone informed without manual effort. Clients can check their session status, upcoming appointments, and outstanding balances directly through the application. Features like automated reminders eliminate the need to send "Are we still on for today?" messages. Your clients receive timely notifications, and you reclaim hours of administrative overhead.

Even parents can log in to view sessions, status updates, and feedback from both teachers and students. Everything lives in one place, accessible to everyone who needs it.

When spreadsheets stop working, Lérins AI is the natural next step. It organizes your relationships so you can focus on what matters most: the work itself.
