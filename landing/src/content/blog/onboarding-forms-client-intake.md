Bringing new students into your business should feel simple — for you and for them. Many teachers and coaches start with Google Forms to collect contact information. It works, but it means your data lives outside your student management system. You end up juggling multiple tools, copying information from one place to another, and hoping nothing slips through the cracks.

Lérins AI's onboarding forms change that. Everything happens inside your client dashboard, from the first click to the first lesson.

## How It Works

You create a form inside Lérins AI. It asks for the information you need — name, phone number, email, or anything else that matters for your intake process. Once the form is ready, Lérins AI gives you a link.

You can share that link anywhere. Post it on Instagram, send it in a Facebook group, include it in a TikTok bio, or paste it into a direct message. When someone opens the link, they see a clean, beautiful page designed to feel welcoming. They fill in their details and press submit.

That is it. Their information appears in your Students section inside Lérins AI — automatically. No manual data entry. No exporting spreadsheets. No copying and pasting.

## Why This Matters

The businesses that grow the fastest are the ones that make it easy for people to raise their hand. An onboarding form is a low-friction way for potential students to say "I am interested." Once they submit, you have their phone number. You can call them, show them your program, and start building a relationship.

Here is how the process typically looks:

1. A potential student finds you on social media.
2. They fill out your onboarding form.
3. You receive their contact information in your Students list.
4. You call them and offer a free introduction to your program.
5. You follow up with a second call to discuss pricing.
6. When they are ready, you enroll them in a class.

This is the moment a contact becomes a student. And with Lérins AI, every step of this journey lives in one place. You track where each student came from, what calls you have had, and when they are ready to enroll. No more switching between Google Forms, a spreadsheet, and your calendar.

## Never Miss a Beat with Status and Notes

As students move through your onboarding process, their status changes. Someone who just submitted the form is a new lead. After your first call, they become a prospect. After the pricing discussion, they are ready to enroll. Then they become an active student.

Lérins AI lets you track this progression with status fields and notes for every student. Open any student profile and you can see exactly where they are in the journey. Add a note after each call — what you discussed, how they reacted, what they need next. Over time, each student builds a完整的 story. You never have to wonder where things stand.

This is especially helpful when you are working with multiple students at once. When a student fills out your form, you see them appear in your list with a clear status. You know who to call next, who is waiting for a follow-up, and who is ready to enroll. Nothing falls through the cracks.

## Send the Right Message at the Right Time

Moving students through the onboarding process takes more than just tracking. Sometimes you need to reach out and nudge them forward. A reminder that their spot is waiting. A quick note to answer a question they had. A friendly invitation to book that first call.

Lérins AI includes email templates designed for exactly these moments. You can send a message with one click — a welcome email when someone fills out the form, a reminder if they went quiet, or a congratulations message when they enroll.

Each template can be personalized with the help of AI. Write a short note about what you want to say, and Lérins AI helps you turn it into a polished email in seconds. You stay in touch without spending hours writing messages. More of your students move through the pipeline and into your classes — because you followed up at the right time with the right message.

## Designed to Be Helpful

Onboarding forms are not about capturing data. They are about starting a conversation. Someone found you online, liked what they saw, and took the time to fill out a form. That is a small act of trust. The form should reflect that — clean, simple, and respectful of their time.

Lérins AI's forms are built the same way. No clutter. No unnecessary fields. Just the information you need to follow up and make a genuine connection.

Whether you are a music teacher, a language tutor, a fitness coach, or run any kind of teaching practice, onboarding forms give you a single place to manage how students find you and how they begin their journey.

Replace your Google Forms. Keep everything in one dashboard. And spend less time managing tools — so you can spend more time teaching.
