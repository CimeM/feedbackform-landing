Post-session wrap-ups are one of the most effective tools for reinforcing what was covered during a session. They improve retention and provide a record of progress. But their value extends far beyond data collection.

The most impactful thing you can do during a wrap-up is acknowledge the human moments. Clients and students often feel self-conscious when receiving feedback about their performance. Starting with something positive is absolutely crucial — they will remember it forever.

I learned this firsthand at a public speaking course. Standing on stage in front of everyone was nerve-wracking. Every mistake felt magnified. But the positive messages I received at the end of each session made all the difference. The self-doubt was still there, but the feedback gave me proof that people wanted me to succeed. Because of that, I did not quit. I kept coming back, even though the course was voluntary and after school hours.

Positive feedback immediately after a session is critical if you want to retain clients. Human connection is what keeps people coming back.

Lérins AI's client dashboard makes this ritual effortless. When you mark a session as complete, the built-in feedback form guides you through documenting both the data and the human moments. Your clients see your notes, and they can share their own perspective. Over time, the feedback history becomes a narrative of growth — one that strengthens your relationship with every entry.
